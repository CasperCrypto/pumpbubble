# Partners — who to ask, what to offer, what to say

Written 2026-10-08. The public page is `/partners`; this is the working list behind it.

## The honest pitch

About **8,000 visitors a month** (the operator's figure, 2026-10), 15 live maps, a Telegram buy bot on
Solana, Base, Ethereum and Robinhood Chain, our own swap. That is small, and the pitch says so: a
partner is not buying reach today. What we can actually give at this size:

1. **Visibility in a place nobody else occupies** — the only free GPU-price strip aimed at crypto
   traders, the only live maps of Robinhood Chain and Base, all next to a map of the asset class.
2. **Integration work, fast** — a feed, a map, a bot feature for their ecosystem, shipped in days.
   Grant programmes pay for exactly that.
3. **Distribution inside Telegram** — every group running the buy bot sees our links on every buy.
4. **Demand** — if Launch Studio is built, it buys GPU time; we can buy it from a partner's network.

What we ask in return, in order of how easily it is given: a co-post on X, a link from their site or
docs, a data feed or permission to show theirs, a grant, a revenue share, money for placement.

**Never offer**: invented traffic numbers, rankings for sale (paid placement is always marked as
paid), anything built on Congress data for a fee (5 U.S.C. 13107).
