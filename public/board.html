The board

How it works

Rank is the bid, nothing else

The board is sorted by how much has been bid. Pay more than the token above you and you pass it. There are no tiers, no editorial, no way to be placed higher by asking.

Held until outbid

A rank does not expire on a timer. It is yours until somebody pays more — which also means the price only ever goes up.

Topping up costs only the difference

Already on the board? You pay the gap, not the full price. Sitting at #2 with 6 SOL against a 10 SOL #1 costs 4.05 SOL to take the top, not 10.05.

Nothing is refunded

Being outbid is the mechanic working, not a fault. Bids are not returned when somebody passes you — your token keeps the bid it paid and the rank it earns.

Every bid is auditable

Payments settle to one public treasury wallet and each confirmed bid carries its Solana transaction. Nothing on this board was placed by us.
