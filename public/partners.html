Questions people actually ask



Does it slow my page down?

It is a lazy-loaded iframe on its own origin, so it cannot block your page. Token art is served
through our CDN cache rather than hotlinked from IPFS.



What happens if my key expires?

The embed keeps working and falls back to the free, badged tier. It never goes blank. A lapsed
invoice should start a conversation, not take your page down.



Can I change what the data says?

No, and that is deliberate. You control presentation — size, timeframe, feed, colour, which token is
pinned. Nothing lets an embed misstate a market cap or a price change, because the moment that is
possible the map is worth nothing to anyone, including you.



Where does the data come from?

pump.fun directly for identity, art and market cap. Price change and liquidity come from
DexScreener. Tokens whose market cap is not backed by liquidity, and copycat ticker swarms, are
filtered by default.
