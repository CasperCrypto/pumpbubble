Free GPU price API

The Compute strip as an open feed: what one GPU rents for, per hour, on Akash's open market, with the day's and week's move. Free to use with a link back to the map.

GET https://pumpbubble.fun/api/compute/gpus
GET https://pumpbubble.fun/api/compute/gpus?history=1   // + hourly points, 8 days

Questions partners ask



  
How big is the audience?
About 8,000 visitors a month, mostly active crypto traders, plus
  the Telegram groups running our buy bot. We are early and say so — partners get placement that is
  priced for today, and grows with us.

  
Can a partner change what the data says?
No. You control presentation — which map,
  colour, pinned token. Nothing lets anyone misstate a price or a market cap, because the day that is
  possible the map is worth nothing to anyone, including you. Paid placement is always marked as paid.

  
Does the embed slow a page down?
It is a lazy-loaded iframe on its own origin, so it
  cannot block your page, and token art comes through our CDN cache.

  
Do you hold users' funds?
Never. The swap and every Buy button hand the trade to the
  user's own wallet to sign.
